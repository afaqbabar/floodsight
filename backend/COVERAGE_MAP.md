## Key Geographic Zones

### 🔴 Critical Bottlenecks (Must Monitor)

1. **Kaub Rhine** (50.08°N, 7.77°E)
   - Narrowest point on Rhine
   - Cargo indicator for all Europe
   - Insurance companies obsess over this point

2. **Passau Danube** (48.57°N, 13.46°E)
   - Three rivers meet (Danube, Inn, Ilz)
   - 2013 flood epicenter (12m water level!)
   - "Three Rivers City"

3. **Ahr Valley** (50.54°N, 7.12°E)
   - 2021 disaster: 220 deaths
   - Flash flood risk (steep valley)
   - High media/emotional value

4. **Hamburg Elbe** (53.55°N, 9.99°E)
   - Europe's 3rd largest port
   - Tidal influence (North Sea)
   - Critical infrastructure

5. **Budapest Danube** (47.50°N, 19.04°E)
   - Capital city (2M people)
   - Frequent floods (2013, 2006, 2002)
   - Government customer
