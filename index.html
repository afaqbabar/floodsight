How FloodSight works

  1. Ingest

    Pulls river gauges, hydrological models, and forecasts from ECMWF, Copernicus, and national agencies.

  2. Enrich

    Normalizes data, adds station metadata, thresholds, and geography for consistent usage across Europe.

  3. Predict

    Runs probabilistic models to estimate exceedance risk and timing for 1–10 day horizons.

  4. Alert

    Emits alerts via webhooks, email, or SMS and updates your dashboards and apps in real-time.
